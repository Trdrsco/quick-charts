# Code of conduct

This code applies to every space the Quick Charts project runs: the repository, its issues and
pull requests, its discussions, and any channel the maintainers open for it.

## Expected behavior

- Write about the code, the report or the proposal, not about the person.
- Assume the reporter is describing what they saw. Ask for the reproduction; do not dispute the
  experience.
- Accept a review as information. Answer the point, change the code, or explain why not.
- Keep what someone shares in a private report private.

## Unacceptable behavior

- Insults, slurs, or personal attacks in any form.
- Harassment, including unwelcome attention that continues after a request to stop.
- Publishing another person's private information.
- Sustained disruption of an issue or review after a maintainer has closed the point.

## Enforcement

Report a concern to the maintainers at the security contact in `SECURITY.md`, or to any maintainer
directly. Reports are read by the maintainers and are not shared beyond them without the reporter's
agreement.

A maintainer may edit or remove a comment, close a thread, or decline a contribution that violates
this code. A person who continues after a warning is removed from the project's spaces. Maintainers
are held to this code by the same process.
